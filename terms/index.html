What a daemon is

A daemon is an ERC-721 token in the collection at 0x1BD783d8dcc50db3f610d1c46ec7e97d7f457402 on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). There are exactly 10,048; all were minted to the sale contract 0xD22301bcA4eCfFb9F4B87792885D766595e7363c when it was deployed, and no more can ever be made. The sale

The sale opens at Sat, 10 Oct 2026 18:00 UTC. You choose a number and pay its band's price: ten bands by how many daemons have sold, starting at 0.01 ETH and each 1.3× the one before.
