Privacy

Daemon · obol.sh · last updated 2026-10-09

obol.sh is run by SHYGUY LLC. This is everything it collects about you.

What we collect

Nothing personal. obol.sh has no accounts, no sign-up, no email capture, no cookies and no analytics. Buying happens between your wallet and the contracts: the page asks your wallet to send a transaction, and your browser — not our server — talks to your wallet.

What's public anyway

Purchases, transfers and every daemon's account are recorded on Robinhood Chain, which is public and permanent. obol.sh reads the chain to show who holds a daemon and what its account holds; it doesn't add to that.

Requests

obol.sh runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests (including your IP address) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy. We don't keep request logs of our own beyond Cloudflare's standard short-lived error logs.

Third parties

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your wallet, Robinhood Chain RPC providers, block explorers and marketplaces have their own policies.
